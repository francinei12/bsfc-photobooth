How the BSFC Photobooth Works

  1. Touch to Start: Tap anywhere on the welcome screen and review the community wall photo notice.
  2. Strike a Pose: Use the live BSFC frame preview and playful pose ideas, then hit Take Photo for a 3-second countdown.
  3. Review & 1 Free Retake: Not loving the first shot? You get 1 retake before printing!
  4. Print & Share: Grab your free 80mm receipt strip (1 print per session, 2-minute cooldown) and scan the QR code or enter your phone number for a full-color digital copy.
